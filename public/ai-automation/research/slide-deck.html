Step 1 · Supply

Two kinds of players sell AI automation

Generalist agencies

Back-office process work: email triage, document extraction, CRM sync, reporting, training. Quoted per project; first projects start around €2,000–€5,000.

Productised software

Front-desk bots: phone receptionists, WhatsApp chat, booking. €35–€299 a month, often bundled into the software a business already uses.
